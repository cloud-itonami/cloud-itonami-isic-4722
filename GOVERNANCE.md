# Governance

cloud-itonami-isic-4722 follows open-source governance with transparent
decision-making documented in ADRs.

Scope: Sales-record (inventory/sale/return) logging, floor-staff
scheduling proposals, supply-order coordination, and compliance-concern
flagging (suspected age-verification failure or over-service) for
specialized beverage retail stores (liquor store, wine shop, craft-beer
shop).

Out-of-scope: Finalizing age-verification overrides, finalizing
responsible-service-of-alcohol decisions, direct point-of-sale
age-verification/ID-scanner actuation, and alcohol-licensing-authority
enforcement (liquor-control-board clearance, license issuance/
suspension, compliance enforcement).

See README.md for complete CRITICAL scope exclusions.
